About Sakwo Resort

Rooted in Sankhu, Open to Everyone

Sakwo Resort is located in Sankhu, on the edge of the Kathmandu Valley — a historic Newar settlement known for its traditional architecture and quiet valley surroundings. Here, guests can combine a relaxing resort stay with time exploring the wider Kathmandu Valley.

Our hospitality is built around comfort and warmth: spacious rooms for families, quiet corners for couples, open spaces for friends, and welcoming venues for celebrations. Whichever way you like to travel, Sakwo is designed to help you slow down.
